**Context:** From the 2026-10-10 architecture review. `planHarvests` in `src/analysis/harvest.ts` is one ~877-line function containing roughly twelve inner closures (`reportUnharvestable`, `paramDomRead`, `unportableNames`, `substituteArgExpr`, its own `selectorFor`/`resolveSelector`/`loopFor` adapters, …) — the largest function in the codebase; the closures are modules without interfaces. No behavior change of any kind.
