**Context:** ADR 0021 s3/s5 and ADR 0026. TC-021 added the composed-markup member to
the escaping union with no producer. This task produces it.

**Do:**

- At a compose site with children, the walk captures the parent-owned region. Its holes
  stay in parent scope, and a `truc:html` inside it is hooked (HTML context) at its own
  site. The capture passes to the include as one more arg in the composed-markup
  context: emitted raw at the insertion element, with no sanitizer hook. SSG never
  sanitizes authored markup, and a target must not either.
- The implicit `owner` arg: minted so that it collides with no prop of any component.
  A compiled compose site that passes children supplies it. Page-rendered instances and
  includes that pass nothing do not. The child writes `data-children` from `owner` only
  when the arg is present. A pass-through forwards it verbatim.
- The dump target's interpreter implements the capture arg and `owner`.
- Retire `not yet emittable` for children passed at a compose site (TC-021 routes them
  Static until this lands).

**Diagnostics:** none new.

**Check:** every corpus composition with children, including one with `truc:html` in
the region and one pass-through, renders byte-identically to SSG through the dump target
for every fixture × locale. A build with no target configured is byte-identical.
